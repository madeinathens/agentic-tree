1️⃣ Hire
Anyone pays 3.25 USDC and enters the doorway. The contract records who paid; the creator sees the payment arrive and engages by hand — 1:1, no automation of the handshake.

2️⃣ Engage (Creator Only)
The creator shakes hands on-chain. This snapshots 70% of every asset as the employer's committed share. From this block until release, the creator CANNOT touch that 70%.

3️⃣ Write & Withdraw
writeLog() — The desk. 420 bytes, public, eternal. Employer or creator writes the pair's deliverables.

employerWithdrawUSDC/ETH/RoundOn() — Withdraw up to 70% of the dynamic pool. Income earned during your term (neuron prompts, Ready Made sales, market moves) grows your share automatically.

resign() — Employer can leave anytime.

release() — Creator can end the term.

Dynamic 70/30 Pool (The Innovation)

The pool is invariant to withdrawals and grows only with income. The employer's share expands as the pair's treasury earns, without any additional action required.
